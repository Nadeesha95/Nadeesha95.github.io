Privacy Policy

YouTube Auto-Dub & 100 Language Subtitles · Last updated: 18 October 2026

In short
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.
  • Your settings and usage counters are stored only on your own device.
  • Only the caption text of the YouTube video you are watching is sent to Google Translate, to translate it and create the dubbed voice.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

1. Who we are

YouTube Auto-Dub & 100 Language Subtitles ("the extension") is a browser extension developed by Nadeesha Sahen Weerasekara ("we", "us"). It translates YouTube videos into the language you choose, showing translated subtitles and/or speaking a voice dub in sync with the video.

2. Information the extension handles

DataWhy it is usedWhere it goes
Caption (subtitle) text of the YouTube video you are watching To translate it into your chosen language and to create the dubbed voice Sent to Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com and translate.google.com). Not sent to us.
Your settings (language, subtitles and dubbing on/off, volume, speed, subtitle style) To remember your choices between sessions Stored only on your device (chrome.storage.local)
Usage counters (minutes dubbed today and this month) Shown to you in the extension popup Stored only on your device (chrome.storage.local)

3. Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses or any other personally identifiable information
  • Your browsing history or the list of videos you watch
  • Google or YouTube account details, passwords or cookies
  • Location, payment or health information

The extension has no servers of its own and sends no data to us.

4. Third-party services

To translate captions and generate the voice, caption text is sent to Google Translate services operated by Google LLC. That processing is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The extension works on YouTube pages, which are covered by YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Permissions the extension uses

  • storage – saves your settings and usage counters on your device.
  • Access to youtube.com – reads the video's captions, shows the translated subtitles and plays the dubbed voice.
  • Access to translate.googleapis.com and translate.google.com – translates caption text and creates the voice.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, other than sending caption text to Google Translate as described above. We do not use data for advertising, profiling or creditworthiness, or for any purpose unrelated to translating and dubbing YouTube videos.

7. Data retention and your control

Settings and usage counters stay on your device until you change them or remove the extension. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data. You can turn subtitles and dubbing off at any time from the extension popup.

8. Children

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
